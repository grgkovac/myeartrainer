My Ear Trainer

Two free trainers for musicians, both running entirely in the browser. Nothing to install, no account, no limit on how much you practise.

My Ear Trainer Hear two tones and say which sat higher — down to a few cents — or name the interval from the tonic. Pitch discrimination · Intervals My Fretboard Trainer Name the note under a dot, or find a named note on the neck. Four- and five-string bass, or guitar. Bass · Guitar · Note names

Why these two

Ear training and fretboard knowledge are the two things that stay awkward longest if you learn an instrument on your own. Both reward short, frequent practice far more than long sessions, which is exactly what a browser tab is good for.

Neither asks for an account or anything to install — open the page and start practising.
